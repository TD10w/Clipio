# Clipio 2.9.1

**[Download Clipio 2.9.1 for macOS (.dmg)](https://github.com/TD10w/Clipio/releases/download/v2.9.1/Clipio-2.9.1.dmg)** · **[下载 macOS 安装包](https://github.com/TD10w/Clipio/releases/download/v2.9.1/Clipio-2.9.1.dmg)**

Open the DMG, then drag **Clipio.app** into **Applications**. / 打开 DMG，将 **Clipio.app** 拖入“**应用程序**”。

- **Try the Drop Zone in Getting Started.** The Collection step now has a sample picture to drag to the top of the screen. The Drop Zone appears, you let go, and the picture lands in Collection, so you know how to collect files from Finder or any other app. The step is optional, and you can delete the picture afterwards.
- The Getting Started window now moves by its top edge, like other Mac windows, so dragging inside it no longer moves the whole window.

## 中文说明

- **在上手引导里试一次拖放收集口。** 「收集区」这一步加了一张示例图片：把它拖到屏幕顶部，收集口会出现，松手后图片就进了收集区。之后从 Finder 或其他 App 收集文件也是同样的操作。这一步可以跳过，图片之后也可以从收集区删掉。
- 引导窗口改为拖动顶部边缘来移动，和其他 Mac 窗口一样；在窗口里面拖动不会再把整个窗口拖走。

Requires macOS 14 or later. Apple silicon and Intel.
