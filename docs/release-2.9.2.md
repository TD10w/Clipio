# Clipio 2.9.2

**[Download Clipio 2.9.2 for macOS (.dmg)](https://github.com/TD10w/Clipio/releases/download/v2.9.2/Clipio-2.9.2.dmg)** · **[下载 macOS 安装包](https://github.com/TD10w/Clipio/releases/download/v2.9.2/Clipio-2.9.2.dmg)**

Open the DMG, then drag **Clipio.app** into **Applications**. / 打开 DMG，将 **Clipio.app** 拖入“**应用程序**”。

- **The Drop Zone now appears for files dragged out of the Dock.** Dragging a file from a Dock folder such as Downloads now brings up the Drop Zone at the top of the screen, just like dragging from Finder. Before, nothing appeared.

## 中文说明

- **从 Dock 拖出文件时，收集口也会出现了。** 从 Dock 里的文件夹（比如“下载”）拖出文件，屏幕顶部会像从 Finder 拖动时一样出现收集口。之前这种情况下收集口不会出现。

Requires macOS 14 or later. Apple silicon and Intel.
